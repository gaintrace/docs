Stripe is where your billing lives, so connecting it gives GainTrace the revenue picture behind every account: what they pay, whether they're renewing, and whether a payment just failed.

## What syncs

GainTrace pulls your Stripe billing objects and ties them to your Companies:

- **Customers** link to the matching company.
- **Subscriptions** feed Revenue, MRR, and renewal dates.
- **Invoices and charges** show payment history and catch failed or overdue payments.

## Connect Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalog">
    Go to **Integrations** in the left nav and pick **Stripe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Paste a Stripe secret key when prompted, then confirm. A restricted key with read access is enough, and keeps the connection scoped to what GainTrace needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Billing data starts flowing in. Manage the connection under **Data > Sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do with it

With Stripe connected, Revenue reflects real MRR and renewal dates, and billing changes become signals: a failed payment or a downgrade can move an account's health and show up in triage before it turns into churn.

## Good to know

- Stripe changes arrive in near real time through webhooks, backed by a regular sync so nothing is missed.
- This connector is read-only. GainTrace reads your billing data and never changes it in Stripe.
