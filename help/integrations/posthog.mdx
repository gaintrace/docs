PostHog knows how customers actually use your product. Connecting it lets that usage drive health, so a drop in active users or stalled adoption shows up as a signal instead of a surprise.

## What syncs

GainTrace pulls these from PostHog and ties them to your accounts:

- **Events** become the product-usage signal behind health and activation.
- **Persons** link usage to the right people and companies.
- **Feature flags, annotations, and cohorts** add context to what changed and when.

## Connect PostHog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalog">
    Go to **Integrations** in the left nav and pick **PostHog**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Paste a PostHog personal API key when prompted, along with your project details, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Your events start flowing in. Manage the connection under **Data > Sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do with it

With PostHog connected, product usage becomes part of health. Adoption trends move scores, a usage drop can raise a signal, and your team sees which accounts are pulling back before renewal.

## Good to know

- PostHog syncs on a regular schedule, so new events land within a few minutes.
- This connector is read-only. GainTrace reads your PostHog data and doesn't change anything in it.
