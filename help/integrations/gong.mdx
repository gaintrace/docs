Gong captures what customers actually say on calls. Connecting it brings that conversation data into GainTrace, so call activity and context sit alongside the rest of an account's story.

## What syncs

GainTrace pulls these from Gong and attaches them to your accounts:

- **Calls** land on the account timeline, so recent conversations are in view.
- **Users** map call activity to your team.
- **Deals** tie conversations back to the pipeline.

## Connect Gong

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalog">
    Go to **Integrations** in the left nav and pick **Gong**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Use **Connect with OAuth** and approve GainTrace in the Gong window. You can also connect manually with API credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Your call data starts flowing in. Manage the connection under **Data > Sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do with it

With Gong connected, call activity becomes part of the account picture. Your CSMs can see recent conversations in context, and a quiet stretch with no calls is one more input into how an account is really doing.

## Good to know

- Gong syncs on a regular schedule, so new calls land within a few minutes.
- This connector is read-only. GainTrace reads your Gong data and doesn't write back to it.
