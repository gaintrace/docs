Mixpanel tracks how people use your product. Connecting it brings that usage into GainTrace, so engagement and adoption become part of every account's health.

## What syncs

GainTrace pulls these from Mixpanel and ties them to your accounts:

- **Events** become the product-usage signal behind health and activation.
- **User profiles** link usage to the right people and companies.
- **Cohorts and funnels** add context to adoption and where customers drop off.

## Connect Mixpanel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the catalog">
    Go to **Integrations** in the left nav and pick **Mixpanel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Paste your Mixpanel credentials when prompted, along with your project details, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it sync">
    Your events start flowing in. Manage the connection under **Data > Sources**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do with it

With Mixpanel connected, product usage feeds health. Adoption trends move scores, a drop in activity can raise a signal, and your team can see which accounts are cooling off well before renewal.

## Good to know

- Mixpanel syncs on a regular schedule, so new events land within a few minutes.
- This connector is read-only. GainTrace reads your Mixpanel data and doesn't change anything in it.
