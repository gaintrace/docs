**Integrations** is the catalog of tools you can connect to GainTrace, under **Integrations** in the left nav. Connecting a source is what fills your Companies, People, and Revenue with real data, so it's usually the first thing an admin sets up.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help/integrations.png" alt="The Integrations catalog grouped by type, with sources you can connect" />
</Frame>

## Browse and connect

The catalog groups sources by type: CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and more), product analytics (Segment, PostHog, Mixpanel, Amplitude), warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake), support (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk), payments (Stripe, Chargebee), and others. Pick a source to connect it. Depending on the tool, you'll authorize it with OAuth, paste an API key, or set up a webhook.

## Manage your connections

Once a source is connected, its data starts syncing into your workspace. You can see and manage everything you've connected under **Data > Sources**.

## Good to know

- Most teams start with their CRM, since that's where accounts and owners live, then add billing and product analytics.
- A few sources in the catalog are marked **Coming soon**. Those aren't connectable yet.
