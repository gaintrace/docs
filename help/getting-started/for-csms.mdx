Once your admin has GainTrace connected, here is how most CSMs use it day to day. There is nothing for you to set up. Your accounts and their data are already here.

## Start in Triage

**Triage** is your queue. It surfaces the accounts that need you today, ordered by what changed and how much it matters, so you are not deciding where to start from a blank page. Work down the list, and once you have handled something, dismiss it or snooze it for later.

## Open a company

Click any account to open its **Company** page. This is the full picture in one place:

- How their health is trending, and what is driving it.
- Recent signals, so you can see what changed and when.
- The people you work with, including who your champions are.
- Revenue, renewals, and any plans in flight.

Spend a minute here before a call and you will walk in knowing exactly where things stand.

## Keep track of your follow-ups

Anything you are on the hook for lives in **Tasks**. Some tasks you create yourself. Others show up automatically from flows your team has set up. Either way, it is one list of what you owe your customers.

## Ask Trace AI

When you would rather ask than click, open **Trace AI** from the button in the corner, or press Cmd J. Ask it things like "which of my accounts have gone quiet this month?" or "draft a check-in email for Acme." It can see your data, so you get a real answer, and it will write outreach you can edit and send.
