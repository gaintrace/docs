Every company in GainTrace carries a health status, so you can tell at a glance who's doing well and who needs a look. You don't set this up. GainTrace works it out from the data flowing in from your connected sources.

## The three states

Health lands in one of three states:

- **Healthy** (green): things look good.
- **At risk** (amber): worth keeping an eye on.
- **Churning** (red): needs attention.

Each company also has a numeric score, so you can compare accounts and sort by it.

## Where you'll see it

- On the **Companies** list, as a colored dot in the Health column. You can filter the list by health, or switch to the board layout to group accounts by state.
- At the top of each **company's page**, alongside a trend that shows how the score has moved recently.

## Good to know

- Health updates as new data comes in, so a score reflects the latest picture rather than a one-time snapshot.
- Health is a guide, not a verdict. Use it to decide where to look first, then open the company for the full story.
