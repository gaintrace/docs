We want everyone your customers ask for feedback to be able to answer, and every teammate to be able to build a survey.

## Standard

We aim for WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

## Scope

This statement covers:

- The survey page opened from a personal invitation link.
- The embedded survey.
- The in-app survey widget (`surveys.js`).
- Survey invitation and reminder emails.
- The survey designer in GainTrace.

It doesn't cover the rest of the GainTrace app, your own pages that host the in-app widget, or custom CSS and brand colors you add.

## Status

GainTrace surveys partially conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Partially conform means some parts don't fully meet the standard yet.

<Warning>
  This is a self-assessment by GainTrace. No third party has audited it, and we haven't yet tested with a screen reader.
</Warning>

The criterion-by-criterion result is in the [Accessibility Conformance Report](/help/surveys/accessibility-conformance-report).

## How we test

- Automated axe-core checks on twelve respondent states that cover ten of the eleven question types (Multiple choice is not yet scanned), against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 Level A and AA rules, in a simulated browser without layout. Color contrast and target size are not checked there.
- Unit tests for keyboard use, focus, announcements, right-to-left layout and the in-app widget.
- A contrast test of invitation emails in dark-mode inboxes.
- Code review against each criterion.

A browser check in Chromium at desktop and phone widths exists, but no run of it is on record yet.

## What works today

- One question per screen, with the question as the heading and focus moved to it.
- Every answer type can be used with a keyboard. Space picks a score and Enter confirms it. Ranking has Move up and Move down buttons.
- Errors are named next to the field.
- The page language and right-to-left direction are set for the survey's language.
- The in-app widget announces itself, closes with Escape and returns focus to your page.
- Email score buttons stay readable in dark-mode inboxes.

## Known limitations

- No screen-reader pass yet with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack.
- Color contrast, target size, reflow and text spacing aren't measured in a real browser yet (WCAG 1.4.3, 1.4.10, 1.4.11, 1.4.12, 2.5.8).
- A brand color you choose sets the selected answer and the focus ring. GainTrace guarantees contrast only for text on that color, not for the color against the page (1.4.11).
- Custom CSS can reduce contrast or hide focus.
- Tapping a score with a mouse or finger moves straight to the next screen, while the keyboard confirms with Enter. Customers can go **Back** to change it (3.2.2).
- The AI follow-up label and its disclosure are always English and aren't marked as English inside a translated survey, (3.1.2).
- Pressing ? opens the GainTrace keyboard shortcuts panel on survey pages and in the survey designer, and it can't be turned off (2.1.4).
- CSAT faces show a word inside each answer, but the accessible name is the number, so speech-input users can't say the visible word (2.5.3).
- The automated scan doesn't yet include the Multiple choice question type.
- The survey designer has no automated accessibility scan yet.

## Technical requirements

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, with JavaScript on. The in-app widget runs inside your page.

## Feedback

If something on a survey or in the survey designer doesn't work for you, email support@gaintrace.com with the survey link and what went wrong.
