## Terms

The terms used in the Conformance Level information are defined as follows:

- **Supports**: The functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects or meets with equivalent facilitation.
- **Partially Supports**: Some functionality of the product does not meet the criterion.
- **Does Not Support**: The majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion.
- **Not Applicable**: The criterion is not relevant to the product.

Level AAA criteria are not part of this report, so no row needs a term beyond these four.

## WCAG 2.x Report

Each Conformance Level below is the lowest one found on any surface in scope. Respondent surfaces means the personal-link survey page, embed, in-app widget and invitation email. Designer means the survey designer.

## Known gaps

The rows marked Partially Supports above are the honest list. The largest gaps are measurement gaps: contrast, target size, reflow, text spacing and resize are checked by code review only, because the browser accessibility check in Chromium (scripts/audit-surveys-ui.ts) has no run on record yet. A screen-reader pass with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver and TalkBack has not been done. The survey designer has no automated accessibility scan. Three further gaps are named in their rows: pressing ? opens the GainTrace shortcuts panel on survey pages and cannot be turned off (2.1.4, Does Not Support), CSAT faces have the number as their accessible name instead of the visible word (2.5.3), and the automated scan does not yet include the Multiple choice question type (1.3.1, 4.1.2).
