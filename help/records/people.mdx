**People** is your directory of the contacts at your accounts, under the **Records** section in the left nav. Like Companies, each person is a resolved record built from your connected sources, so you get one row per contact rather than a duplicate from every tool.

## Find a contact

- **Search** matches on a person's name, email, or company.
- **Sort by** orders the list by name, engagement, or last seen.
- The **Filters** button narrows by name, email, company, or role.

## Read the list at a glance

Each row shows the person, their email, the company they belong to, their role, an engagement score, and when they were last seen. Click a company name to jump straight to that account.

## Spot your champions

Contacts marked as champions show an amber star next to their name, so you can pick out your key relationships as you scan. Open a person to see their record, recent activity, and champion tracking if it's turned on for them.

## Good to know

- People are created from your connected sources. There's no manual add step; a contact appears once their data has synced.
- **Export** downloads your contacts as a CSV.
- The list shows up to 500 people at a time, so on large workspaces use search and filters to get to the ones you need.
- Some fields like email or phone can be hidden for your role if your admin has set field policies, so you may not see every value.
