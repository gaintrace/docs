**Boards** are configurable dashboards, under **Boards** in the left nav. Each board is a set of widgets you arrange yourself, and every widget draws from your real workspace data.

## Create a board

Use **New board** and pick **Blank board**, or start from a template like Executive overview, Health & risk, Revenue & retention, or Adoption & engagement. Click the title to rename it.

## Add widgets

Open a board and choose **Add widget**. In the builder you give the widget a name, pick a **Chart type**, and choose what it measures two ways:

- **Preset**: a ready-made customer-success metric like ARR under management, Net or gross revenue retention, average health, or at-risk ARR.
- **Build your own**: pick a data source, a measure (count, sum, average), and how to group it.

You get a live preview as you go, and there are chart types for most shapes of data, from a single number to lines, bars, pie, funnel, gauge, table, and a map.

## Arrange and share

Drag widgets by their header to reorder them, drag an edge to resize, and set the date range for the whole board from the top. Use **Share** to set who can see a board: keep it **Restricted**, open it to your **Workspace**, or make it **Public**.

## Good to know

- A widget only shows data it has a source for. A metric with no connected source stays empty until that data is flowing.
- The **Reset** button in the editor replaces the current widgets with a default set, so use it to start over rather than to undo a single change.
